About
📡
Action4Water
A free satellite tool to help groups screen water areas for possible algal activity.
🛰️
What it does
Uses Sentinel-2 imagery to flag possible elevated algal activity in lakes and rivers.
⚠️
What it does not do
It does not test drinking-water safety or replace field sampling and laboratory analysis.
📡
Data source
Satellite imagery processed through Google Earth Engine using Sentinel-2 SR data.
✉️
Contact
Email the project team for questions or community use: contact@action4water.org
